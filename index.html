Our origin

From one real workflow to a bigger opportunity.

01 · THE START

A studio website

Work began with the digital presence of SCULP Studio, a bridal beauty business in the Toronto area.

02 · THE DISCOVERY

Operations need context

The work expanded into SCULPY, connecting bookings, people, conversations and daily operations. We noticed familiar problems that many small teams face.

03 · THE PRODUCT

Memora takes shape

We began translating the approach into a reusable CRM, starting with an independent car dealership demonstration and planning broader service-industry use cases.

What we're exploring

AI should adapt to people.
Not the other way around.

Our focus is reliable natural-language intake, structured extraction with human review, conversational retrieval, and practical business reporting. We're exploring Claude API integration as part of that development.
